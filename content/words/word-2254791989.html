ᴱQ. kekainen num. card. “hundred (when not multiplied)” (Category: Large Cardinal Number)

⚠️ᴱQ. cecainen, num. card. “hundred (when not multiplied)” (Category: Large Cardinal Number)

A variant word for “a hundred” from the Early Qenya Grammar (EQG) of the 1920s appearing as keakai(li) [manuscript version] or kekainen [typescript version] as a combination of ᴱQ. kea “ten” and ᴱQ. kainen “hundred” (PE14/50, 83). It was used “when not multiplied”, so presumably as a stand-alone word as opposed to larger numbers like ᴱQ. yúyo tuksa “two hundred”.
