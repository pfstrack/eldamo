G. uf¹ prep. “out of, forth, from” (Category: Out, Outside)

⚠️G. uf¹, prep. “out of, forth, from” (Category: Out, Outside)
S. ed¹ “out, out of, [N.] forth”

A word in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “out of, forth, from”, said to be the equivalent of ᴱQ. ū (GL/74). The closest form in the contemporaneous Qenya Lexicon is ᴱQ. ū “beneath” under the early root ᴱ√Ū¹ “under” (QL/96), but the Gnomish word hints at a different root, perhaps *ᴱ√UVU. Compare G. gôf versus ᴱQ. yáva < ᴱ√ẎAVA (GL/38; QL/105).
