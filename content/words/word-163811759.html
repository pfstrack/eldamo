ᴹQ. yuima pron. “of both sorts” (Category: Two)

⚠️ᴹQ. yuima, pron. “of both sorts” (Category: Two)
ᴺQ. !yuyaitë “of both sorts”

A correlative combination in Demonstrative, Relative, and Correlative Stems (DRC) from 1948 appearing as ᴹQ. {yúyina >>} yuima (PE23/107), a combination of ᴹQ. yúya “both” and ᴹQ. -ima “sorts”. It replaced deleted {yúyina} (PE23/107 note #92).

Neo-Quenya: For purposes of Neo-Quenya, I would update this to ᴺQ. yuyaitë “of both sorts” using the later suffix -itë.
