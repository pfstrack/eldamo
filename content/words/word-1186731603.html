ᴺS. !paenel num. card. “thirteen” (Category: Teen Cardinal Number)

ᴺS. !paenel, num. card. “thirteen” [created by Elaran] (Category: Teen Cardinal Number)

A neologism for “thirteen” proposed by Elaran posted to the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) on 2018-01-16, inspired by Q. quainel which was a variant of Q. nelquë “thirteen”. Elaran proposed a full paradigm of Sindarin “teen” numbers following this pattern. He and Fiona Jallings also proposed an alternate form neleb that was a more direct cognate to Q. nelquë, but I prefer paenel since I feel it is easier to recognize.

S. pae “ten”
√NEL “three”
