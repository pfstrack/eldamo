ᴺQ. !naima n. “grievous matter, pity, shame” (Category: Shame)

ᴺQ. !naima, n. “grievous matter, pity, shame” [created by Luinyelle] (Category: Shame)

A neologism for “grievous matter, pity, shame” coined by Luinyelle, posted on 2026-05-27 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) and based on NAY¹ “bitter grief or pain”. It can be used in the phrase yé naima “too bad, what a pity, what a shame”.
