ᴺQ. !muimë n. “secret” (Category: Secret)

ᴺQ. !muimë, n. “secret” [created by Valerie] (Category: Secret)

A neologism for “a secret” coined by Valerie in 2024 posted on 2026-06-06 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a noun form of [ᴹQ.] muina based on the same root ᴹ√MUY. Muimë is a general word for “secret” with no positive or negative connotations, as opposed to nulu which is a “dark secret”.
