      G. bodra- v. “to hinder, delay, tarry” (Category: to Delay)

      A verb (both transitive and intransitive) appearing as G. bodra- “hinder, delay, tarry” in the Gnomish Lexicon based on G. bod “behind, back, of time ago” (GL/23).

      Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would instead use ᴺS. hebia- “to delay, retain, keep (temporarily)” based on √KHAP “retain, keep, detain”. This neologism arose in a conversation on Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) from 01-25-2020.
