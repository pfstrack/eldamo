ᴺQ. !hairië n. “distance, (lit.) farness” (Category: Far)

ᴺQ. !hairië, n. “distance, (lit.) farness” [created by ABNW] (Category: Far)

A neologism for “distance” appearing in Ales Bican’s New Words (ABNW) from the early 2000s, a noun form of [ᴹQ.] haira “remote, far”, so more literally “farness”. Petri Tikka instead suggested hayassë “distance” in Parma Penyanë Quettaron (PPQ) also in the 2000s, but I prefer hairië.
