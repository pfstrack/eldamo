S. Perhael-adar “Sam-dad”

S. Perhael-adar “Sam-dad”

A form of address spoken by Elanor to her father Samwise in the Unpublished Epilogue to the Lord of the rings, a combination of his Sindarin name Perhael with adar “father” (SD/127). In the manuscript draft Elanor instead used Panthail “*Fullwise” (SD/135).
