ᴱN. sairos n. “great age” (Category: Age)

⚠️ᴱN. sairos, n. “great age” (Category: Age)
ᴺS. ^ioros “(great) age, eld, oldness”

A word appearing as G. horoth “age, eld, oldness” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s, a noun form of G. hôr “old, aged, ancient” (GL/49). The Early Noldorin Dictionary of the 1920s had ᴱN. sairos “great age” (PE13/165).

Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would update this word to ᴺS. ioros “(great) age, eld, oldness” using the later word S. iaur for “old, ancient”.
