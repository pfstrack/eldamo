ᴺQ. !lúvu n. “bow (knot with two loops)” (Category: Knot)

ᴺQ. !lúvu, n. “bow (knot with two loops)” [created by Laiqua] (Category: Knot)

A neologism for “bow” as a “knot with two loops” (such as when tying shoelaces) coined by Laiqua posted on 2026-04-18 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), dual form of lúva “[one] bow”. Orondil suggested that colloquially vilwarin might also be used for such a bow-knot.

Q. lúva “bow (in forming tengwar), bight, bend, curve” dual
