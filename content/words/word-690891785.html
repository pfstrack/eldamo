ᴺQ. !lumbarya- v. “to tire, wear out” (Category: Tired, Weary)

ᴺQ. !lumbarya-, v. “to tire, wear out” [created by Sanwë] (Category: Tired, Weary)

A neologism for “grow tired” coined by Sanwë posted on 2025-11-11 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), an inceptive verb form of lumba “weary”, so more literally “become tired/weary”, with half-strong past lumbanse. The thing that wearies you is in the ablative, so lumbaryan “I weary” vs. lumbanses i vantallo “he grew tired from the march”.

Elements

ᴹQ. lumba “weary”
Q. -arya “*inceptive”
