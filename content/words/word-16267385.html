ᴺQ. !epello adv. “already, (lit.) from after” (Category: Ready)

⚠️ᴺQ. !epello, adv. “already, (lit.) from after” [created by Helge Fauskanger, PPQ] (Category: Ready)
Q. tensi “already, still, up to now/here; yet [from present]”

A neologism coined by Helge Fauskanger in Parma Penyanë Quettaron (PPQ) from the early 2000s, an ablative form of epë “after” so more literally “from after”. In his Neo-Quenya New Testament (NQNT) Fauskanger instead used [ᴺQ.] nollo, an ablative form of nó “after” so more literally “from before”.

Elements

Q. epë “after (of time), following; before (in all relations but time)”
Q. -llo “(movement) from; ablative suffix”
