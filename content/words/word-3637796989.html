ᴺQ. !tor-, v. “to roast (intr.)” [created by Laiqua] (Category: to Cook)

A neologism for “roast” coined by Laiqua posted on 2026-05-10 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), based on the early root ᴱ√TORO having to do with hot things. It is intransitive, for example: i hrávë tóra ruimenessë “the flesh roasts in the fireplace”.
