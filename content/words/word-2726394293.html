S. [ndr] become [nr] before another [d]; [ndrSd] > [nrSd]

Tolkien mentioned this sound change in the notes from the late 1960s: “findarātō would have become find-raud > findrod > finrod with loss of the medial d before a following d” (VT41/9). A similar phenomenon might have applied to N. anrand from the The Etymologies of the 1930s.

Order (05800)

After 05500 [au], [ae] became [o], [e] in polysyllables ✶findarātō > find-raud > findrod > S. finrod VT41/9
Before 05900 middle consonants frequently vanished in clusters
