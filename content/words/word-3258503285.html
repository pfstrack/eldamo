ᴺQ. !necië n. “division, separation, parting” (Category: to Separate)

ᴺQ. !necië, n. “division, separation, parting” (Category: to Separate)

Helge Fauskanger proposed the neologism ᴺQ. ciltië “division, separation” for his Neo-Quenya New Testament (NQNT) based on ᴺQ. cilta- “to divide, separate”. I prefer ᴺQ. nec- “to divide, separate, part”, and would thus update this neologism to necië “division, separation, parting”; see that entry on ᴺQ. nec- for discussion.

Elements

!nec- “to divide, separate, part; [with ablative] quit, give up, part with, separate oneself from, break up with”
