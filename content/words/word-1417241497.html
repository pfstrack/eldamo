ᴱQ. limbelu adv. “*long” (Category: Long)

⚠️ᴱQ. limbelu, adv. “*long” (Category: Long)

A word appearing in Sí Qente Feanor from the 1910s, in the phrase ᴱQ. i limbelu tuktalla nai mára which might mean “*the long search may be good” (PE15/32, 37, 40).
