S. iavas n. “autumn” (Category: Autumn)

S. iavas, n. “autumn, *harvest [time]” (Category: Autumn)
G. gavon “harvest (time)”
G. thafwen “hay, harvest”

The usual Sindarin word for “autumn”, equivalent to Q. yávië, as well as the Sindarin name of the corresponding 54 day period in the calendar of Imladris (LotR/1107; SD/129; PM/135). It may also mean “*harvest [time]” like its Quenya equivalent, and is clearly based on the same root ᴹ√YAB “fruit”.
