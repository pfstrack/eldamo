G. hadra- v. “to face, oppose, stand in way; to resist, thwart” (Category: Defeat)

⚠️G. hadra-, v. “to face, oppose, stand in way; to resist, thwart” (Category: Defeat)
ᴺS. !dadhra- “to face, oppose, stand in way; to resist, thwart”

The Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s had a verb G. hadra- “face, oppose, stand in way; resist, thwart” based on the preposition G. had meaning “opposite” or in combination with the allative meaning “against” (GL/47).

Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin I would update this verb to ᴺS. dadhra- “to face, oppose, stand in way; to resist, thwart” based on the later preposition S. dan “back to, against”.

Reference ✧ GL/47 ✧ “face, oppose, stand in way; resist, thwart”

Elements

had “facing, opposite, over against; (c. allative) against, instead of, in return for, for, as” ✧ GL/47
