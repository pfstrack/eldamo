ᴺQ. !palantyassa, adj. “popular, widely liked” [created by Arael] (Category: to Please)

A neologism for “popular, widely liked” coined by Arael posted on 2025-11-05 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of and palan “wide(ly)” and an adjectival form of tyaz- “to like” (tyassa < tyan-s-a).

Elements

Q. palan “far (and wide), afar, distant”
ᴹQ. tyaz- “to like”
