G. restu adv. “out of land, abroad, away from home” (Category: From, Away)

⚠️G. restu, adv. “out of land, abroad, away from home” (Category: From, Away)
S. palan “afar, abroad, far and wide”

An adverb in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “out of the land, abroad, away from home”, apparently based on ᴱ√redh- (GL/65). Immediately beneath it was a deleted form redhos “land”, probably also related and perhaps with an incomplete gloss.
