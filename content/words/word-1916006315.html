ᴱN. garadren adj. “having room for, convenient, adequate, opportune” (Category: Enough)

⚠️ᴱN. garadren, adj. “having room for, convenient, adequate, opportune” (Category: Enough)
ᴺS. ^garadhren “having room for, convenient, adequate, opportune”

A word appearing as ᴱN. garadren “having room for, convenient, adequate, opportune” in the Early Noldorin Dictionary of the 1920s, likely based on ᴱN. gair- “there is room (for)” and related to ᴱN. garth “place” (PE13/161).

Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would update this to ᴺS. garadhren based on later S. gardh “region”.
