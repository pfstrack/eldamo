ᴱN. crithui adj. “circular” (Category: Round)

⚠️ᴱN. crithui, adj. “circular” (Category: Round)
ᴺS. ^rend¹ “circular”

A word for “circular” in Early Noldorin word-lists of the 1920s (PE13/141), apparently an adjective form of ᴱN. craith “circle” < ᴱ✶kṛkta from the same document (PE13/140).

Conceptual Development: The Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s had G. crithog “circular” vs. noun G. crithos “a circle” (GL/27). Gnomish Lexicon Slips modifying that document instead had G. crithiol “circular” (PE13/111).
