See ᴹQ. évea for discussion.

      ᴹQ. évea adj. “future” (Category: After)

      An adjective appearing as ᴹQ. évea “future” used only of persons and places based on the future form éva “will exist” of the verb [ᴹQ.] ea- “be, exist” (PE22/123). It referred to a remoter time than tulurya.

      Neo-Quenya: In Tolkien’s later writings, the future of ëa- was euva, so for purposes of Neo-Quenya I would update this adjective to ᴺQ. euvëa, as in i euvëa aran “the future king”.
