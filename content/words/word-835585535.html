ᴺQ. [ᴱQ.] ^arcólima adj. “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able” (Category: Equal, Alike)

ᴺQ. [ᴱQ.] ^arcólima, adj. “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able” (Category: Equal, Alike)
See ᴱQ. aryúlima for discussion.

Related

  • Q. imya “same, identical, self-same” would, for ordinary use, be a better choice than ^arcólima “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able”

Elements

Q. ar- “by-, beside, near”
Q. cólima “bearable, light (of burdens and things comparable, troubles, labors, afflications)”

Cognates

  • ᴺS. !asgolui “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able”

Derivations

  • < !ᴺ✶askōlimā “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able”

      ᴱQ. aryúlima adj. “equal, equivalent” (Category: Equal, Alike)

      A Quenya word appearing as ᴱQ. aryúlima in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s given as the cognate to G. argulthion “equal, equivalent” (GL/20), clearly based on ᴱQ. yulu- “carry” (GL/38) and meaning something like “*beside-bear-able”.

      Neo-Quenya: For purposes of Neo-Quenya, I would update this word to be based on later col- “to bear, carry”, and thus: ᴺQ. arcólima “equivalent, (lit.) beside-bear-able”.
