ᴺS. !govai adj. and adv. “alike, similar” (Category: Like, Similar)

ᴺS. !govai, adj. and adv. “alike, similar” [created by Paul Strack] (Category: Like, Similar)
G. feltha “similar, like”
G. gofeltha “*similar, like”

Sindarin equivalent of Q. ovéa “(con)similar, alike”, derived from primitive *wōbeya. Sami Paldanius instead suggested ᴺS. gothiol “similar” based on a combination of go “together” and ᴹ√THĒ “seem”.

          G. gofel adj. and adv. “exactly alike” (Category: Equal, Alike)

          A word appearing as gofel, gofil, or gwanin “exactly alike” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s (GL/40, 43), a combination of G. go- “together” and G. fel “like”. It had a deleted variant {gwavin} (GL/44).
