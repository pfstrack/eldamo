ᴺQ. !(y)ondarma- v. “to collide, crash together” (Category: Lie, Falsehood)

ᴺQ. !(y)ondarma-, v. “to collide, crash together” [created by Luinyelle] (Category: Lie, Falsehood)

A neologism for “collide, crash together” coined by Luinyelle posted on 2025-11-03 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of o- “together” and ᴹ√DARAM “beat, [ᴱ√] batter”. For the collision of two things it would be ondarma-. For the collision of multiple things it would yondarma- with the prefix yo- “together (of three or more things)”.

Elements

Q. o- “together”
