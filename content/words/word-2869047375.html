ᴺS. !farthannen adj. “sated, satisfied” (Category: Enough)

ᴺS. !farthannen, adj. “sated, satisfied” (Category: Enough)
See G. fadin for discussion.

Elements

!fartha- “to sate, satisfy” passive-participle

      G. fadin adj. “sated, saturated” (Category: Enough)

      An adjective in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “sated, saturated” based on G. fad “enough” (GL/33).

      Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would update this word to ᴺS. farthannen as the passive participle of a verb ᴺS. fartha- “to sate, satisfy”.
