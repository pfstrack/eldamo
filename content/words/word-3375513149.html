ᴺQ. !súleta- [þ] v. “to nitpick, (lit.) make [use] þúle” (Category: Shame)

ᴺQ. !súleta- [þ], v. “to nitpick, (lit.) make [use] þúle” [created by Luinyelle] (Category: Shame)

A neologism for “to nitpick” coined by Luinyelle posted on 2025-11-05 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a causative verb based on the letter-name þúle whose modern pronunciation is súle. The pronunciation of this verb can indicate whether the speaker agrees with the nitpicking. If pronounced súleta the speakers feels the nitpick is unreasonable, while if pronounced þúleta the speaker agrees the nitpick is justified.

Elements

Q. súlë “breath; (movement of) spirit, emission of power (of will or desire)”
