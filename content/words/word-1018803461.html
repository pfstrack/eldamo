ᴱN. thain adj. “level, even” (Category: Flat)

⚠️ᴱN. thain, adj. “level, even” (Category: Flat)

A word appearing in an untranslated phrase in the Early Noldorin Grammar of the 1920s: ᴱN. tha i·’lathros an i bailchir beleg thain, but this last word was deleted (PE13/128 and note #75). The word thain also appeared in contemporaneous Early Noldorin word-lists, with the glosses “level, even” and a primitive form ᴱ✶stainá (PE13/153).
