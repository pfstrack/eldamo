Q. enwina adj. “old” (Category: Old)

Q. enwina, adj. “old” (Category: Old)

A word for “old” appearing in the version of the Markirya poem from the 1960s (MC/222). It might be related to Q. enwa “yet”.

Conceptual Development: The ᴱQ. Oilima Markirya poem from around 1930 had ᴱQ. aire “old”. The Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s had similarly formed ᴱQ. haura as the cognate of G. hôr “old, aged, ancient (only of things still existing)” based on primitive ᴱ✶χou̯-ră (GL/49).
