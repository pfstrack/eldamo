      G. hortha- v. “to grow old” (Category: Old)

      A word appearing as hortha- “grow old” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s, a verb form of G. hôr “old, aged, ancient” (GL/49).

      Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would update this word to ᴺS. iortha- “to grow old, *age” using the later word S. iaur for “old”.
