ᴺQ. !rucule n. “mess, disorder” (Category: to Destroy)

ᴺQ. !ruculë, n. “mess, disorder” [created by Arael] (Category: to Destroy)

A neologism for “mess, disorder” coined by Arael posted on 2025-11-03 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) inspired by rúcina “confused, shattered, disordered, ruined”. Luinyelle instead suggested torcotun “mess, shambles” as a combination of torco “troll” and tundo “hole” (lit. = “troll-hole”), but that seems too metaphorical to me and I also recommend against using tundo = “hole” given that later on tundo meant “hill” instead.
