G. gofelweg n. “one’s fellow or equal, rival” (Category: Equal, Alike)

⚠️G. gofelweg, n. “one’s fellow or equal, rival” (Category: Equal, Alike)

A word appearing as gofelweg or felweg in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s translated “one’s fellow or equal, rival” (GL/34, 40), a combination of G. go- “together”, G. fel “like”, and the masculine suffix -weg.
