ᴺQ. !tengwaitë, n. “literate, able to read or write” [created by Parmandil] (Category: to Read)

A neologism for “literate” coined by Parmandil posted on 2026-01-14 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of tengwa- “read” with the suffix -itë “capable of doing”.

Elements

Q. tengwa- “to read written matter”
Q. -itë “adjectival ending; [with verbs] capable of doing, generally (and naturally) doing”
