S. Cerveth n. “July” (Category: Names of the Months)

S. Cerveth, n. “July” (Category: Names of the Months)

Name of the seventh month of the year in the Westron calendar (LotR/1110) corresponding to “July” (PM/135). Its meaning is unclear.

Conceptual Development: In drafts of The Lord of the Rings appendices, this name was Cerfeth and it appeared with an alternate name Cadlaer or Cadloer “*After Summer” = cad “*after” + S. laer or N. loer “summer” (PM/135-136).
