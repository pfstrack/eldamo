ᴺS. !adbannad(a)- v. “to refill” (Category: Full)

ᴺS. !adbannad(a)-, v. “to refill” [created by Paul Strack] (Category: Full)

A neologism coined by Paul Strack in 2021 specifically for Eldamo, a combination of ad- “re-” and [N.] pannad(a)- “fill”. Ryszard Derdzinski instead proposed ᴺS. adbanna- in Parf Pith ’Wain (PPW) from the early 2000s, but it was based on an incorrect form of the verb “fill”, since the notion of Sindarin/Noldorin half-strong verbs was poorly understood at that time.

Elements

S. #ad- “back, again, re-”
N. #pannad(a)- “to fill”
