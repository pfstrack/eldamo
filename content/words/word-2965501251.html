ᴺS. [G.] ^caethas n. “causation” (Category: Cause, Reason)

ᴺS. [G.] ^caethas, n. “causation” (Category: Cause, Reason)
See G. caithos for discussion.

Elements

^caeth “cause, reason, motive”

      G. caithos n. “causation” (Category: Cause, Reason)

      A word appearing as G. caithos “causation” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s based on G. caith “cause, reason, motive” (GL/24).

      Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would update this to ᴺS. caethas “causation” based on ᴺS. caeth “cause, reason, motive” as an updated form of G. caith.
