ᴱQ. diéra adj. “yesterday’s; (mostly in sense) bygone, over, passed” (Category: Yesterday)

⚠️ᴱQ. diéra, adj. “yesterday’s; (mostly in sense) bygone, over, passed” (Category: Yesterday)
Q. nöa “yesterday; former, *previous; ⚠️tomorrow”
Q. vanwa “gone, lost, departed, vanished, past, over, no longer to be had, passed away, dead, [ᴹQ.] gone for good; ⚠️[ᴱQ.] on the road”

An adjective for “yesterday’s” in the Qenya Lexicon of the 1910s based on the noun ᴱQ. die “yesterday”, along a variant ᴱQ. ’yenda or ’yendina based on longer noun ᴱQ. ’yenye(n) “yesterday” (QL/105). The short form diéra is mostly used in the sense “bygone, over, passed”.
