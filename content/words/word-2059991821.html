      G. felthadrin adj. “probable” (Category: Like, Similar)

      A word appearing as G. felthadrin “probable” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s, an adjective form of G. feltha- “seem likely, be probable” (GL/34).

      Neo-Sindarin: Since I update G. feltha- to ᴺS. biltha-, I would update felthadrin to ᴺS. bilthadren “probable, likely”.
