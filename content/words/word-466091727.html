ᴱN. goneb adj. “similar” (Category: Like, Similar)

⚠️ᴱN. goneb, adj. “similar” (Category: Like, Similar)
ᴺS. !govai “alike, similar”

An indeclinable adjective meaning “similar” in the Early Noldorin Dictionary of the 1920s based on ᴱN. neb “near” (PE13/164).

Reference ✧ PE13/164 ✧ “similar”

Elements

neb “near” ✧ PE13/164

      G. gofel adj. and adv. “exactly alike” (Category: Equal, Alike)

      A word appearing as gofel, gofil, or gwanin “exactly alike” in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s (GL/40, 43), a combination of G. go- “together” and G. fel “like”. It had a deleted variant {gwavin} (GL/44).
