ᴱQ. úva adj. “the under” (Category: Down, Below, Beneath)

⚠️ᴱQ. úva, adj. “the under” (Category: Down, Below, Beneath)
ᴺQ. !unda “lower, beneath”

The Qenya Lexicon of the 1910s had the adjective ᴱQ. ūva “the under” under the early root ᴱ√Ū¹ (QL/96).

Neo-Quenya: Luinyelle and Arael coined a neologism ᴺQ. unda “lower, beneath” as an adjectival form of undu “down, under”, posted to the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) on 2022-05-23.
