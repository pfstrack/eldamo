ᴹQ. tulurya adj. “future, coming” (Category: After)

ᴹQ. tulurya, adj. “future, coming” (Category: After)
ᴺQ. !apalúmë “future, (lit.) after-time”
ᴱQ. núora “tomorrow’s, to be; future”
ᴺQ. !tulya “coming, approaching, future”

A word for “future, coming”, an imperfect/active participle of ᴹQ. tul- “come” using the suffix ᴹQ. -lya dissimilated to r after an l (PE22/107, 123). It has an implication of a nearer future, as opposed to ᴹQ. évea for remoter futures.

Neo-Quenya: In Tolkien’s later writing, the active participle suffix was -(i)la (PE22/154-155), but I think tulurya might be retained as an inceptive formation = “*beginning to come”, especially in reference to the near future. If you dislike this, you could instead use ᴺQ. tulya “coming, approaching, future”, a neologism proposed by Röandil in a 2023-02-28 post to the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS).
