ᴺQ. !lúpan- v. “to schedule, (lit.) arrange in time” (Category: Time (other))

ᴺQ. !lúpan-, v. “to schedule, (lit.) arrange in time” [created by Arael, Luinyelle] (Category: Time (other))

A neologism for “schedule” coined by Arael and Luinyelle, posted on 2026-01-14 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) as a combination of [ᴹQ.] lú “time” with pan- “arrange”.
