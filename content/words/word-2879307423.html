ᴺQ. !eccahasta-, v. “to hatch, incubate [eggs], (lit.) sit them out” [created by Arael] (Category: Action)

A neologism for “to hatch, incubate” for eggs coined by Arael posted on 2026-04-18 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of a frequentative form of √KHAD “sit” with the prefix √ET “out” [*et-khakhad-da], so more literally “sit them out”.
