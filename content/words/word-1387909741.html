ᴱQ. yunqekainen num. card. “one hundred twenty”

⚠️ᴱQ. yunquecainen, num. card. “one hundred twenty”

A variant word for “one hundred twenty” from the Early Qenya Grammar (EQG) of the 1920s as a combination of ᴱQ. yunqe “twelve” and ᴱQ. kainen “ten”, following the pattern of words like ᴱQ. kan(ta)kainen “forty” (PE14/50, 83). Compare more ordinary ᴱQ. yukainen tuksa.
