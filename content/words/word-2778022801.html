ᴺQ. !tittavëo n. “micro-organism” (Category: Bit, Small Thing)

ᴺQ. !tittavëo, n. “micro-organism” [created by NQ-Wiki] (Category: Bit, Small Thing)

The Neo-Quenya Wiki suggested ᴺQ. mitsavëo for “micro-organism” as a combination [ᴹQ.] mitsa “small” and vëo “living thing”. Since mitsa was rejected by Tolkien, I would update this word to tittavëo using [ᴹQ.] titta “tiny” instead.

Elements

ᴹQ. titta “little, tiny”
Q. vëo “living creature”
