ᴺS. !ilnad pron. “everything” (Category: All, Every)

ᴺS. !ilnad, pron. “everything” [created by Fiona Jallings, FJNS] (Category: All, Every)

A neologism for “everything” coined by Fiona Jallings as a combination il “all” and [N.] nad “thing”. Elaran instead proposed ᴺS. illad based on the sound change whereby ancient ln became ll. I prefer Fiona’s neologism, since I think the sound change of ln to ll is ancient enough that it is no longer active in modern Sindarin and thus ln is possible as a combination in later compounds. However, there are no attested examples of ln in Sindarin or Noldorin of the 1950s and 60s, so illad is probably more popularly among Neo-Sindarin writers.
