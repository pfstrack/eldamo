ᴺQ. !imyalë n. “sameness” (Category: Equal, Alike)

Element In

      ᴱQ. inqile n. “sameness” (Category: Equal, Alike)

      A noun appearing as ᴱQ. {inqele >>} inqile “sameness” in the Qenya Lexicon of the 1910s base on the adjective ᴱQ. inqa “same” under the early root ᴱ√IMI¹ (QL/42).

      Neo-Quenya: For purposes of Neo-Quenya, I would update this word to ᴺQ. imyalë “sameness” based on the later adjective Q. imya “same”.
