G. ribin adj. “striped, edged” (Category: Line)

⚠️G. ribin, adj. “striped, edged” (Category: Line)

A word in the Gnomish Lexicon appearing as G. ribin “striped, edged”, an adjectival form of G. rib “a stripe, line; border, fringe”, equivalent to less common G. rimin¹ of the same meaning (GL/65).
