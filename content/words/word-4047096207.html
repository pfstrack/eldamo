ᴱN. garadros n. “opportunity” (Category: to Happen)

⚠️ᴱN. garadros, n. “opportunity” (Category: to Happen)
ᴺS. ^garadhras “opportunity”

A word for “opportunity” in the Early Noldorin Dictionary of the 1920s, a noun form of ᴱN. garadren “having room for, convenient, adequate, opportune” and related to ᴱN. garth “place” (PE13/161).

Neo-Sindarin: Since I update ᴱN. garadren to ᴺS. garadhren, I would update the noun form to ᴺS. garadhras “opportunity”.
