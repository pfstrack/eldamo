ᴺQ. !olvangolmë, n. “botany” [created by Vyacheslav Stepanov] (Category: Knowledge, Science)

A neologism for “botany” coined by Vyacheslav Stepanov posted on 2026-02-20 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of olva “plant” and nolmë “knowledge”.
