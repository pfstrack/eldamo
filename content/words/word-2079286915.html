ᴺQ. !nattusta, n. “situation, (lit.) collection of affairs” [created by Luinyelle] (Category: Thing)

A neologism for “situation” coined by Luinyelle posted on 2025-10-03 in the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), a combination of [ᴱQ.] natto (nattu-) “affair” and the collective suffix -sta.
