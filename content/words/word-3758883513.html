ᴺQ. !veryalë n. “challenge, dare” (Category: to Dare)

ᴺQ. !veryalë, n. “challenge, dare” [created by Paul Strack] (Category: to Dare)

A neologism for “challenge, dare” coined by Paul Strack specifically for Eldamo based on attested [ᴹQ.] verya- “to dare”. In a 2025-12-18 post to the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS), Orondil instead suggested ᴺQ. vertalë “challenge, dare” base on hypothetical transitive ᴺQ. verta- “to dare”, but I see no reason to introduce a new verb form. Luinyelle instead suggested ᴺQ. orolahtalë “challenge” in the sense of a “difficult or terrifying task” in a 2026-03-31 post to the Vinyë Lambengolmor Discord Server (VLDS) based on oro “mountain” and lahta- “cross” so more literally “mountain-crossing”, but I think that derivation is too specialized for the more general meaning of a “challenge”.
