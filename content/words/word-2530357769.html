G. cîrin adj. “present (place or time), modern” (Category: Today)

⚠️G. cîrin, adj. “present (place or time), modern” (Category: Today)
ᴺS. ^cýren “present (time), modern”

A word in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “present (place or time), modern” based on G. cî {“now” >>} “here”, but its gloss was deleted (GL/26).

Neo-Sindarin: For purposes of Neo-Sindarin, I would adapt this word as ᴺS. cýren “present (time), modern” as a combination of √KEW “new, fresh” with the adjective suffix -ren.
