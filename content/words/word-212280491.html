ᴺS. !abor¹ n. “tomorrow, (lit.) after-day” (Category: Tomorrow)

ᴺS. !abor¹, n. “tomorrow, (lit.) after-day” [created by Fiona Jallings] (Category: Tomorrow)
G. nôn “tomorrow, the next day on from one mentioned or in thought, the morrow”

A neologism for “tomorrow” coined by Fiona Jallings as a combination of ab “after” and aur “day”. Gábor Lőrinczi instead suggested ᴺS. ordolel in the VinQuettaParma Wiki (VQP) as a combination of aur “day” and tolel “coming”, the active participle of tol- “come”.

Elements

S. #ab “after [of time]”
S. aur “(whole) day”
