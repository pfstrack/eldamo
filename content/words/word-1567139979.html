Q. enwa adv. “yet” (Category: Time (other))

Q. enwa, adv. “yet” (Category: Time (other))

A word in the first version of the Löa Yucainen poem form 1958 in the phrase yallume Eldalie enwa marnie endoresse luminke “in times when the Eldalie dwelt yet a little while in Middle-earth” (CPT/1296-7). It is probably related to Q. en(a) “still”, also from the late 1950s, which was derived from √EN “again, go on doing” (PE17/167).
