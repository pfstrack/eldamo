ᴺQ. !cacátië n. “design” (Category: Form, Shape)

ᴺQ. !cacátië, n. “design” [created by NQ-Wiki] (Category: Form, Shape)

A neologism for “design” coined by Paul Strack in 2025 specifically for Eldamo, a noun form of ᴺQ. cacátu- “to design”. The Neo-Quenya Wiki instead proposed ᴺQ. nó “design” probably based on NŌ “beget; create”, but that conflicts with too many other words.

Elements

!cacátu- “to design, shape or fashion slowly”
