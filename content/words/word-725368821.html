ᴺQ. !etetulië n. “outcome” (Category: End (n, temporal))

ᴺQ. !etetulië, n. “outcome” [created by NQ-Wiki] (Category: End (n, temporal))

A neologism for “outcome” appearing in the Neo-Quenya Wiki, a combination of et(e)- “out” with a noun form of tul- “come”. In VinQuettaParma Wiki (VQP), Tamas Ferencz instead suggested ᴺQ. virda “outcome, result” as a combination of vir (< √WIS) “change” and -dā “product of an action”, but the phonological result of *wis-dā is actually vista, which conflicts with [ᴹQ.] vista “air”.

Elements

Q. #et(e)- “*out”
Q. tul- “to come”
