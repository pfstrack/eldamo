ᴺS. !sa², prep. and adv. “beside, alongside, next (to)” [created by Paul Strack] (Category: Preposition)
G. edh “outside, near borders of, near, hard by, beside”
G. lent “near, close to, up by, along side of; (c. dat.) at, towards, up to side of; therewith, with it, withal”

A neologism coined by Paul Strack in 2018 specifically for Eldamo based on an inverted form of the root √AS “beside”. This derivation is rather dubious, but I have found no better alternatives.

Derivations

      G. art adv. “beside, along side of” (Category: Near)

      A word in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “beside, along side of” related to G. ar “at” (GL/19-20).

      • < ᴱ√ASA “*beside, close to”

          G. artha prep. “alongside (of)” (Category: Near)

          A word in the Gnomish Lexicon of the 1910s glossed “alongside (of)”, probably based on G. art “beside, along side of” (GL/20). It was followed by a cryptic comment “of motion to, c. all.”, which according probably indicates that when combined with the allative this word means “motion to[wards]”.
